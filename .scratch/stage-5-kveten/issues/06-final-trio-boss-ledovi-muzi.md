# 06: Finální trio boss: Ledoví muži (Pankrác, Servác a Bonifác)

**What to build:** At the 300-second climax of Stage 5, sudden folkloric frost strikes the May meadows: the Ice Saints trio—Pankrác, Servác, and Bonifác—appear in heavy winter sheepskin coats (kožuchy). They orbit the player in a synchronized triad, turning Lada's warm spring landscape into frosty blue ice wherever they step. Pankrác throws fans of sharp icicles, Servác summons ground-targeted hailstone AoE circles (krupobití), and Bonifác emits a freezing pulse that turns nearby XP gems into spiky ice hazards that damage the player until thawed. Defeating the trio breaks the unseasonal frost, restores May's sunshine, unlocks Stage 6, and awards the Ice Saints trophy.
