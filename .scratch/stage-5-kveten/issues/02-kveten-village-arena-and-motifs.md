# 02: Májová aréna a motivy z ladovské ilustrace Květen

**What to build:** The player explores an authentic May village arena that preserves the exact layout, dimensions, and collision footprint of Stage 1 (cottages, church, tavern, school, pond, stream, mill, and forest groves), but is fully transformed by May seasonal motifs drawn from Josef Lada's "Květen" calendar illustration:
- An ornate outer cartouche border with the top "KVĚTEN" banner, blooming lilac clusters, crawling cockchafers, and Gemini cherubs.
- Village square with the tall decorated Maypole (Májka) and circle dancers in traditional Bohemian folk costumes.
- Wayside crossroads shrine dedicated to Sv. Jan Nepomucký providing a protective sanctuary aura.
- Warm, fully thawed water in the stream and pond with water lilies and reeds (no winter ice floes).
- Pastoral POIs including the upper sheep pasture with the flute-playing shepherd, children wading by the stream, and goat groves in bloom.
- Seasonal weather effects featuring soft May rain showers and floating floral petals.
