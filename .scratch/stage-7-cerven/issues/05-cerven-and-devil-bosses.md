# 05: Pekelní a červnoví bossové (Vele-rak, Pekelný čert sekáč a Královská Polednice)

**What to build:** The player confronts three thematic boss encounters scaling up to 300 seconds of intense survival: at 85 s, the Miniboss Royal Crayfish (`kralovsky_vele_rak`) with impenetrable frontal carapace and mud surges; at 185 s, the Midboss Devil Reaper (`cert_sekac`) wielding a massive flaming scythe with 180-degree cleaves and igniting dry hay; and at 300 s, the Climax Boss Queen Polednice (`kralovska_polednice`) commanding blinding heat waves, swirling grain dust storms, summoning devil cohorts, and shifting into a St. John's summer thunderstorm.

**Blocked by:** 02: Geometrie arény Červen a letní body zájmu, 04: Taktická červnová havěť a 2 nová hejna (+200 % obtížnost)
